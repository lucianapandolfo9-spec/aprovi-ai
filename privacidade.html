Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

aprovi.ai is a content approval and scheduling service operated by Luh Panda (CNPJ 66.521.744/0001-60, Brazil). Agencies and brands use it to get client approval on posts and to publish approved content to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube at the scheduled time. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Account operators: agency or brand staff who sign in to the aprovi.ai panel.
  • Approvers: clients who review posts through a private approval link.
  • Connected social accounts: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook accounts whose owner authorized aprovi.ai to publish on their behalf.

2. Data we collect

  • Panel account: email address used to sign in.
  • Content: videos, images, captions, titles and scheduling times uploaded for approval and publishing.
  • Approval records: approval decisions, caption versions, the publishing options chosen for each post (such as privacy level and whether comments are allowed), and timestamps.
  • TikTok, when you connect an account through Login Kit: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues to aprovi.ai. We also read the creator information TikTok provides before each post (for example, the privacy options available to your account).
  • YouTube, when you connect a channel through Google sign-in: your channel ID and name, and the OAuth tokens Google issues to aprovi.ai.
  • Publishing results: the IDs, links and status returned by each platform after a post is sent.

We do not collect your TikTok or Google password, your followers, your messages, or analytics about your audience.

3. How we use the data

  • To show content to the right approver and record their decision.
  • To publish, at the scheduled time and with the options chosen, only content that has been approved, to the account the owner connected.
  • To show the publishing status of each post and to alert the operator when something fails or a connection needs to be renewed.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use connected-account data to train AI models.

4. Google and YouTube data

aprovi.ai uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

aprovi.ai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request only the permission needed to upload videos to the channel you connect.

You can revoke aprovi.ai's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. TikTok data

TikTok data is used only to publish content you approved to the TikTok account you connected, as allowed by the TikTok Developer Terms of Service. You can disconnect aprovi.ai in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services.

6. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL), with access restricted by row-level security and server-side functions.
  • Access and refresh tokens are kept encrypted in Supabase Vault. They are never shown in the panel and never sent to the approver's browser.
  • Media files are kept while the brand uses aprovi.ai (some are reused in recurring stories) and are deleted under the retention rules below.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the services needed to run aprovi.ai: Supabase (hosting and database), and the platform you chose to publish to (TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta), which receives the content and options you approved. We may disclose data if required by Brazilian law.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are deleted when you disconnect an account in aprovi.ai or revoke access on the platform.
  • Posts and approval records are kept while the brand is active and deleted within 30 days after the brand's contract ends, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data at any time by email (below). We answer within 15 days, as provided by Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

9. Children

aprovi.ai is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify account operators of material changes.
